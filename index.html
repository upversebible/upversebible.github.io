Common questions

  • How do I add the widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search UpVerse and pick a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then tap the box under the time.
  • How do I change the Bible language? Open the You tab and tap Bible language.
  • How do I restore UpVerse Plus? Open the You tab and tap Restore purchases.
  • How do I cancel Plus? Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → UpVerse Plus.

Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

UpVerse does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (XP, levels, quests, bookmarks, saved verses, language and settings) is stored only on your device. We never see it, and it is deleted if you delete the app.

Purchases

UpVerse Plus and one-time gifts are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing

When you share a verse, it goes through Apple's share sheet to the app you choose. UpVerse does not send it anywhere else.

Children

UpVerse is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
